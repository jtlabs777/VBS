The Theme

Wonderfully Made

Wonderfully Made is a joyful, hands-on Vacation Bible School designed to help children discover that they are created in God’s image and deeply loved by their Creator. Through engaging Bible lessons, movement, creativity, and exploration, children will learn that their bodies are a gift from God—His living temple—and that caring for them is an act of gratitude and worship.

🍎

Healthy Recipes

Hands-on food preparation and tasty healthy recipes that nourish the body.

🏃

Exercise & Fun

Exciting movement and physical activities designed for every child.

🌱

Gardening

Learning the miracle of growth and how to cultivate what we eat.

🧬

Body Design

Discovering the incredible ways God designed the human body to work.

📖

Story Lessons

Faith-filled, story-based lessons that inspire and educate.

🎨

Crafts & Activities

Creative projects and hands-on fun that reinforce the daily themes.

🚑

First Aid & CPR Instruction

A special feature on how to respond to emergencies through introductory First Aid and CPR instruction.
